OUR STORY

Built by faith.
Held by family.

City Word Family Church is a community in Antipolo where every generation can know Jesus, find belonging, and live with purpose.

WELCOME HOME

We believe the local church should feel alive with grace, truth, joy, and genuine relationships.

Our story is not about a building. It is about people saying yes to God, making room for others, and carrying hope into homes, schools, workplaces, and communities.

Whether you are exploring faith for the first time or have followed Jesus for years, you are invited to grow alongside us.
